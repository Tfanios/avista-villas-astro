01 · The Pitches

Avista, in three lengths

When someone asks "what is Avista?", every team member should have a version ready. One sentence for a party, one paragraph for an email, one page for a journalist.
01

The one-liner

"Three private villas in the pines of Sithonia, kept by us for families who want Greece on their own terms."

02

The paragraph

Avista is a small collection of three private villas in Sithonia, Halkidiki — the Avista Private Resort, our contemporary stone flagship, and two warmer sister villas: the Avista Villa with its private pool, and the Avista Superb Villa without one. Same family, three ways to spend a quiet week in Greece.

03

The full story

Avista began as a single stone house in a clearing between the pines of Sithonia. Over the years, it became a small collection — the Avista Private Resort (the contemporary stone flagship), the Avista Villa (a traditional Greek family home with a private pool), and the Avista Superb Villa (its sister property, same warm architecture, no pool). Sithonia itself is the quieter middle leg of Halkidiki: forested, unhurried, closer to how Greece felt twenty years ago. Our guests are mostly families who are finished with big-resort holidays — who want a private home, a hand-drawn map of the best local beaches and tavernas, and a small team they can reach directly. We keep the houses ourselves, because it's the only way they stay this quiet.

Style rules

All icons 40×40px grid, 1.2px stroke, rounded line-caps, no fill. Always charcoal (#2B2A27). Never tinted, never coloured, never bold.

Where they belong

Villa detail pages (amenities grid). Booking confirmation emails. Welcome book section openers. Never in navigation. Never alone without a text label.

10 · Seasonal Flex

The brand through the year

The palette stays the same, but the emphasis shifts with the season. This keeps the Instagram feed fresh and the website photography rotation meaningful.

High summer

June · July · August

The primary palette, at full volume. Aegean blues allowed at 5%. Bougainvillea moments OK. All three villas shown regularly. Headlines about cicadas, slow mornings, the sea.

Olive season

September · October

The best-kept secret. Warmer tones come forward — terracotta, deep stone, olive-harvest greens. Fewer pool shots, more kitchen and garden. Headlines about harvest, figs, the quieter beach.

Quiet months

November → April

The Instagram grid slows down. Charcoal and cream lead. Stories instead of new posts. Content about the land, the stone, the houses at rest. Headlines about next summer.

11 · Examples

Side by side

The easiest way to teach a brand is to show both sides of the line.

Do this

"A week of nothing is still a week well spent."
Serif type on cream. One idea. Generous space. The italic does the work of emphasis.
Three houses.
One quiet Greece.
Olive background, cream serif. Four words. The feel of a printed page.

Not this

✨ BOOK NOW!! 20% OFF ✨
Gradient background. Emoji. All caps sales urgency. Comic-style typeface.
⭐ LUXURY VILLA EXPERIENCE ⭐
World-class amenities!!
"Luxury" said out loud. "World-class" and "amenities" — the three words Volume I asks us not to use.
A useful test

"Would I put this on the kitchen table?"

Before publishing any post, printing any card, or sending any email — ask this. A piece that belongs to Avista is one a guest would happily find on the kitchen table of the villa they're staying in.
